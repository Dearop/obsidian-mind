# North Star

A living document of goals, aspirations, and current focus areas. Both you and Claude write to this. Claude reads it at the start of meaningful work sessions and references it when making suggestions.

## Current Focus

_What am I working toward right now?_

- Finishing my Master’s in Cybersecurity strongly, with work I’m genuinely proud of  
- Building deep technical taste in systems, cryptography, and security  
- Putting myself in a position where I can choose between research, high-end engineering, and startup opportunities  
- Working on projects that are both technically serious and actually useful

## Goals

### Short-term (This Quarter)

- Successfully finish my master’s project  
- Build a clear picture of what kind of work energizes me most: research, startup building, or elite engineering  
- Leave this period with at least one project/work artifact I can point to with pride

### Medium-term (This Half)

- Graduate with strong technical credibility  
- Strengthen my portfolio around difficult, rigorous technical work  
- Find or create an opportunity where I can work with exceptionally strong people on meaningful problems  
- Become more consistent, calmer, and more deliberate in execution

### Long-term (This Year+)

- Successfully finish my master’s degree in cybersecurity  
- Build a career around technically deep, high-agency work  
- Either join or help build something excellent in security, cryptography, systems, or adjacent infrastructure  
- Develop into someone people trust with hard problems

## Aspirations

_What kind of engineer/person am I becoming?_

- Detail-oriented  
- Meticulous in work  
- Thoughtful  
- Patient  
- Rigorous  
- Reliable  
- Hard to shake under pressure  
- Someone who combines depth with execution  
- Someone who does not cut corners intellectually

## Anti-goals

_What am I explicitly NOT optimizing for?_

- Prestige without substance  
- Busywork  
- Shallow output that looks good but has no depth  
- Working with people who are not serious, honest, or competent  
- Chasing opportunities that pull me away from mastery  
- Starting something just to say I have a startup
