# Intake Questions

> [!success] Resolved 2026-04-13
> All intake questions answered. Decisions captured below and propagated to [[Research Agenda]], [[Overview]], source template, and CLAUDE.md research conventions.

## Decisions

### Scope
- **Domains**: Mathematics, Cryptography, Computer Science, AI — all long-term interests.
- **Breadth**: As broad as possible. Not limited to SNARK/STARK.

### Sources
- **Types**: Research papers, articles, books, podcasts, lecture notes, meeting notes — all accepted.
- **Paper ingests**: Full formal breakdown with mathematical elements and important formulae. Rigorous, ground-truth focused. No hand-waving.
- **Lighter sources**: Separate, lighter workflow acceptable for web articles vs papers.
- **Ingest cadence**: One source at a time with review. No batch processing.

### Organization
- **Primary axis**: Concepts (not people, not projects, not courses).
- **Frontmatter**: Rich — for easy indexing and querying.
- **Tags**: Heavy use.
- **Output format**: Markdown notes only.
- **Literature maps**: Yes, as dedicated synthesis pages.

### Maintenance
- **Lint frequency**: Every 10 ingested sources.
- **Suggestions**: LLM should actively suggest missing sources and next papers to read.
- **Contradictions**: Track explicitly in [[Contradictions]]. Review and clear during lint passes.

### Workflow
- **Purpose**: Reading comprehension, long-term retention, project ideation, literature reviews — all.
- **Auto-synthesis from queries**: No.
