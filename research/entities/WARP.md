# WARP

## Who / what it is

WARP is a hash-based **accumulation scheme** introduced in *[[WARP Linear-Time Accumulation Schemes]]*.

Its headline property is being the first accumulation scheme with **linear prover time** and **logarithmic verifier time**.

## Relevance to this wiki

WARP is the current anchor for the vault's recursion / composition branch.

It matters because it is not merely another SNARK, but a paper about the **machinery used to build recursive proof systems efficiently**, especially in PCD/IVC settings.
