# Fractal

## Who / what it is

Fractal is a **post-quantum transparent recursive-proof methodology / system** introduced in *[[Fractal Post-Quantum and Transparent Recursive Proofs from Holography]]*.

Its defining idea is to obtain recursion by compiling **holographic IOPs** into **preprocessing SNARKs**, and then compiling those into **preprocessing PCD**.

## Relevance to this wiki

Fractal is currently the strongest bridge note between:
- the IOP / holography branch,
- the transparent proof-system branch,
- and the recursion / PCD branch.

A useful memory hook is:
- **Fractal = post-quantum transparent recursion from holography + preprocessing.**
