# Srinath Setty

## Who / what it is

Srinath Setty is the author of *[[Spartan Efficient and General-Purpose zkSNARKs without Trusted Setup]]* and a recurring researcher in succinct proof systems and zero-knowledge arguments. Affiliated with **Microsoft Research**.

## Relevance to this wiki

Setty appears to be an important figure for the user's emerging interest in SNARK/STARK-style proof systems, especially around:
- practical proof-system design,
- transparent zkSNARKs,
- R1CS-based systems,
- and the interface between theory and implementable systems.
