## Key Work

Working on the **EVM precompile layer for post-quantum signature verification** — part of Ethereum's Post-Quantum L1 north star in the Strawmap. The mechanism: introduce lattice-based (FIPS-204/ML-DSA) signature verification precompiles so wallets can transition to quantum-safe auth via account abstraction without touching the core protocol.

Key proposal in this space: **EIP-8141** targeting the **Hegotá** hard fork (H2 2026) — signature agility through account abstraction. The EF's coordination hub is [pq.ethereum.org](https://pq.ethereum.org), with 10+ client teams on weekly PQ interoperability devnets.
