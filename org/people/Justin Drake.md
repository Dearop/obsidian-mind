## Connection to Paul

Met at ZKProof 8, Rome, May 9 2026. Paul asked which EIPs to tackle for summer open source work. Justin's recommendations:

1. **EIP-8250 — Keyed Nonces for Frame Transactions**: Replaces single sender nonce with per-key independent nonce sequences. Unblocks privacy protocols; Vitalik's entry point to Ethereum state scaling (hundreds of billions of entries). Authors: Vitalik, Toni Wahrstätter, Lightclient, Thomas Thiery.
2. **EVM pre-compilation of PQ crypto** — linked to [[Antonio Tolas]]. See EIP-8141 targeting Hegotá (H2 2026).
